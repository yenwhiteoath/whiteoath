## Marxism reading list

-   [Vladimir Ilyich Lenin - Karl Marx (A Brief Biographical Sketch With an Exposition of Marxism)](https://www.marxists.org/archive/lenin/works/1914/granat/ch02.htm)
-   [Vladimir Ilyich Lenin - On the Question of Dialectics](https://www.marxists.org/archive/lenin/works/1915/misc/x02.htm)
-   [Leon Trotsky - The ABC of Materialist Dialectics](https://www.marxists.org/archive/trotsky/1939/12/abc.htm)
-   [J. V. Stalin - Dialectical and Historical Materialism](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/stalin/works/1938/09.htm)
-   [Frederick Engels - Socialism: Utopian and Scientific](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1880/soc-utop/int-mat.htm)
[General Introduction and the History of Materialism] + [Part II: [Dialectics]](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1880/soc-utop/ch02.htm)
-   [Frederick Engels - Ludwig Feuerbach and the End of Classical German Philosophy](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1886/ludwig-feuerbach/index.htm)
-   [Frederick Engels (1877) - Anti-Dühring](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1877/anti-duhring/)
    -   Mostly for part 1 - philosophy, especially chapter 10 (dialectics)
-   [Frederick Engels - The German Ideology [Part 1 Chapter A. Idealism and Materialism]](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1845/german-ideology/ch01a.htm)
-   [Mao Tse-tung - On Contradiction](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-1/mswv1_17.htm)
-   [Louis Althusser (1962) - Contradiction and Overdetermination](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/althusser/1962/overdetermination.htm)
    -   "Althusser also writes on what separates Marx from Hegel here"
-   [Frederick Engels (1873-1886) - Dialectics of Nature - Chapter 2](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1883/don/ch02.htm)
